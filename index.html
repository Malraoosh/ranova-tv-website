Ready in three steps

Everything is set up on the TV with your remote. No account, no website sign-up.

  1. Add your source

    Enter an M3U playlist URL, with an optional XMLTV guide, or your Xtream server and login.

  2. Let it load

    Ranova TV reads your channels, movies, series, and programme guide, then refreshes them on the schedule you choose.

  3. Press play

    Browse by category, zap by channel number, or search across every source you have added.
